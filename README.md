## What the project does

    The project is an opening trainer for chess. It helps people learn different chess openings, but having them first play the opening while it tells you what moves to make with arrows, and then after that tests you by making you play against the same moves with no guidance.

## How to use it

    First you should select an opening you want to play. There are numerous openings for both white and black. Then for each opening go through the process of first learning the opening, and then testing yourself by playing the opening with no other help. Finally once you have completed all the lines, you can start a final test where you will play against all the openings you learnt, and see if you can recollect how to play against them.

## Which features I am most proud of

    I think I like the overall workflow a lot. It allows for a good ammount of learning and the whole process is one that drills the person on the opneing, and allows them to actually learn how to play it, in a way that they will remember during their games.
